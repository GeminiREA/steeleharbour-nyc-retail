Platform Overview

Institutional discipline in a market most capital overlooks

New York City retail has reset. Marginal tenants have been cleared out, quality space is scarce, and demand from food and beverage, fitness, wellness and necessity-based operators is strong. Yet assets in the $10–50 million range are too small for most institutions and too complex for most private buyers.

SteeleHarbour's NYC Retail Investment Platform is built for that gap. We pair local underwriting and leasing expertise with the capital markets and structuring capability of a firm that has acquired approximately $2.0 billion of assets since 2019.

Strategy

Three ways we create value

Core+ Acquisitions

Well-located, leased street retail with durable tenancy and room for operational improvement, underwritten to both in-place income and re-leasing economics.

Value-Add Repositioning

Re-tenanting, lease restructuring, reconfiguring awkward or oversized space, activating cellar and second-floor area, and monetizing signage and frontage.

Structured Capital

Credit, preferred equity and mezzanine positions where speed and creative structuring give us an edge with owners and lenders.

Why NYC Retail, Why Now

Fundamentals have improved faster than pricing

Several forces converge in the current cycle to favor disciplined, well-capitalized buyers with local execution experience.

Constrained supply

New retail construction has slowed sharply and aging inventory has been removed, creating a durable floor under rents and occupancy for well-located space.

Resilient tenant demand

Restaurants, fitness and wellness operators favor long leases and heavy build-outs, which makes them resistant to e-commerce and sticky once in place.

A dislocated size segment

Sub-$50 million assets attract less institutional capital, and many trade well below prior peaks even as rents in top corridors have recovered.

Motivated sellers

Loan maturities, extended and modified debt, and estate liquidations are producing off-market and lightly marketed opportunities.

Deep, global demand

Record visitation, a full return to the office, and one of the world's largest concentrations of wealth continue to support foot traffic and spending.

Conservative capital structure

Modest leverage lowers risk and emphasizes durable cash flow, so returns come from the real estate rather than from financial engineering.

Investment Approach

What we look for

  • Fee-simple street-level retail units and mixed-use buildings with a substantial retail component
  • Single-tenant and multi-tenant assets across New York City
  • Each asset underwritten to dark value and re-leasing economics, not only in-place cash flow
  • Tenant-in-hand strategies ahead of acquisition or lease expiration
  • Off-market sourcing through long-standing relationships with lenders, brokers and owners
