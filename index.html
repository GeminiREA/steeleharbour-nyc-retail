SteeleHarbour Overview

A privately held investment manager, founded in 2018

  • Institutional-grade asset management, underwriting, reporting and accounting resources applied to every investment
  • Seeks superior risk-adjusted returns by investing in GP equity and structured fixed-income investments
  • Multifamily and mixed-use focus through development and value-add; other sectors, including retail, pursued opportunistically
  • $332mm+ of retail and mixed-use development transactions executed by the SteeleHarbour senior team, including ground-up and master-planned projects with national-credit tenants
  • ~$100mm of aggregate capital commitments invested across all SteeleHarbour managed vehicles
  • ~$1.4Bn of assets under management assembled across 25 investments closed since firm inception in 2018
